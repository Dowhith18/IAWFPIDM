RPM Over Time

Engine load vs. ideal operating range

Staying in the 1k–3k RPM band keeps the engine inside its efficient torque curve. Extended redline operation amplifies heat, fuel use, and wear.

  • › Above 5k RPM indicates aggressive acceleration or downshifts.
  • › 1k–3k RPM is the ideal cruise envelope for most vehicles.
  • › Prolonged idling below 1k RPM wastes fuel without progress.

Speed Analysis

Cruise behaviour across the drive session

The highlighted zone emphasises efficient cruising speeds (roughly 50–100 km/h) where aerodynamic drag and engine load remain balanced.

  • › Above 100 km/h drag climbs quickly, impacting economy.
  • › Target 50–100 km/h for relaxed expressway driving.
  • › Sub-50 km/h suggests congestion or frequent stops.

Acceleration Trends

Throttle inputs and braking cadence

Smoother pedal inputs keep torque delivery predictable and boost passenger comfort while lowering wear on brakes and tyres.

  • › High spikes call out hard acceleration or braking.
  • › Gentle slopes mean predictive driving and coasting.
  • › Monitor repeated oscillations for potential traffic.
